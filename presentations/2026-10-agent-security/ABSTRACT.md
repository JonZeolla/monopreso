# Agent Security

**BNY Cyber Spark** · October 23, 2026 · Virtual · 30 min

--

Agents can now act on our behalf, call each other, ship tons of code, and maybe even directly touch production data. How do we make sure that's all happening safely, and aligned with our requirements?

As you can imagine, there's a lot of work happening in this space. This talk will focus in on one of the most complex and fast moving: identity. We'll cover many of these new approaches like AAuth, DPoP, and EMA, and contrast those with OAuth 2.1, the standard that many protocols like MCP and A2A are building on (even though it's still in draft).

Walk away with an understanding of both the benefits and drawbacks of each of these approaches and how to get started with them today.
